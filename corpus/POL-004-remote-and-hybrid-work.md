# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy describes the three work arrangements at Fernhill Technologies, who is eligible for each, and what is expected of employees who work away from an office. It applies to full-time and part-time employees and to interns.

This policy covers your regular, ongoing work arrangement within your country of employment. Working temporarily from a different state, province or country is covered by the Working From Another Location Policy (POL-005). The two policies are meant to be read together.

## 2. Work Arrangements

Every employee has one of three work arrangements recorded in their employee profile.

### 2.1 Office-based

An office-based employee works from their assigned office on every working day. Office-based roles are those that need on-site presence, such as office management, facilities and some IT support roles. Office-based employees may work from home occasionally with their manager's agreement, up to 2 days per month.

### 2.2 Hybrid

A hybrid employee works from their assigned office on anchor days and may work from home on other days. Hybrid is the default arrangement for employees who live within 80 kilometers (50 miles) of a Fernhill office.

### 2.3 Fully remote

A fully remote employee works from a home office and is not required to attend an office regularly. Fully remote employees must still live in their country of employment, and in a state or province where Fernhill is registered as an employer (see section 6).

## 3. Anchor Days for Hybrid Employees

### 3.1 Anchor days

Hybrid employees attend the office on at least 2 anchor days per week. The anchor days are Tuesday and Thursday in every office. Teams may agree a third in-office day, but a manager cannot require more than 3 in-office days per week for a hybrid employee.

### 3.2 Missing an anchor day

An anchor day may be missed for PTO, sick leave, a company holiday, approved business travel, or time worked from another location under POL-005. Otherwise, a hybrid employee who needs to miss an anchor day should agree it with their manager in advance. Repeatedly missing anchor days without agreement is a performance matter handled by the manager.

### 3.3 Interns

Interns are hybrid by default and attend the office on at least 3 days per week, including both anchor days, so that they can learn alongside their team.

## 4. Eligibility for Fully Remote Work

### 4.1 Who is eligible

An employee is eligible to be fully remote if all of the following are true:

- The role can be performed remotely, as confirmed by the department director.
- The employee has completed the probationary period, or was hired into a role advertised as fully remote.
- The employee's most recent performance review rating is "Meets expectations" or higher, where a review exists.
- The employee has a home workspace that meets the requirements in section 5.

### 4.2 How to request a change of arrangement

To move between hybrid and fully remote, raise a ticket in the HR portal under the category "Work Arrangement Change". The request needs approval from your manager and your department director. People Operations confirms that the location is one where Fernhill can employ you. Allow 15 business days for a decision.

### 4.3 Review

Work arrangements are reviewed once a year. Fernhill may change an arrangement with 60 calendar days of written notice if business needs change.

## 5. Home Workspace Requirements

Employees who work from home on any day must have:

- A reliable internet connection with a download speed of at least 25 megabits per second.
- A private area where confidential conversations cannot be overheard and screens cannot be seen by others.
- A way to store company equipment securely when it is not in use.
- A safe working environment. Fernhill may ask you to complete a home workspace self-assessment once a year.

You must follow the Information and Data Security Policy (POL-006) at all times, including the rules on company devices, the VPN and working in public places.

## 6. Where You May Live

### 6.1 Country of employment

You must live in your country of employment. Moving your home to a different country is not a work arrangement change. It is an international transfer, which requires a new employment contract and is considered only when there is a business need. Speak to People Operations before making any plans.

### 6.2 Registered states and provinces

Fernhill is registered as an employer in the following places:

- United States: Colorado, Texas, Washington, Illinois, New York, Florida and Oregon.
- Canada: Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta.
- Portugal: all regions.

### 6.3 Moving home within your country

If you plan to move your home to a different state or province, raise a ticket under "Work Arrangement Change" at least 30 calendar days before the move. Moves to a state or province on the registered list are normally approved. Moves to a state or province that is not on the list need a tax and payroll review, which can take up to 60 calendar days, and may be declined.

Your pay may be adjusted when you move, according to the pay zone for your new location. People Operations will tell you the effect before you commit to the move.

## 7. Equipment and Costs

### 7.1 Equipment

Fernhill provides a company laptop and standard accessories to every employee, whatever their work arrangement. See the Equipment and IT Asset Policy (POL-008).

### 7.2 Home office stipend and internet

Hybrid and fully remote employees are eligible for a one-time home office stipend after the probationary period. Fully remote employees also receive a monthly internet allowance. The amounts and rules are set out in the Expense Reimbursement and Travel Policy (POL-007) section 6.

### 7.3 Travel to the office

Travel between home and your assigned office is a personal commuting cost for hybrid employees and is not reimbursed. For fully remote employees, travel to an office at Fernhill's request is business travel and is reimbursed under POL-007.

## 8. Availability and Communication

Remote and hybrid employees keep the core hours in POL-001 section 5.2, in the time zone of their assigned office. Keep your calendar up to date and your status visible in the company chat tool. Attend team meetings with your camera on where practical.

## 9. Fully Remote Employees Visiting an Office

Fully remote employees are expected to attend in person for team gatherings, normally up to 4 times per year. Fernhill gives at least 30 calendar days of notice and pays travel costs under POL-007.

## 10. Temporary Changes

Short-term changes to where you work, for example working from a relative's home in another state for two weeks, are not a change of work arrangement. They are covered by POL-005, which sets limits on how long you may work from another state, province or country and what approval you need.

## 11. Questions

Raise a ticket in the HR portal under the category "Work Arrangement Change" for requests, or "Policy Question" for general questions. See POL-013 for response times.
