# Outside Activities, Conflicts of Interest and Public Communications Policy

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains what you may do outside your Fernhill job, how to declare a conflict of interest, who owns work you create in your own time, and how to speak about Fernhill in public and on social media. It applies to full-time employees, part-time employees and interns. Independent contractors follow the terms of their contract, and sections 6 to 9 apply to them when they speak about their work for Fernhill.

The basic rule on conflicts of interest is in the Workplace Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy (POL-012) section 6. This policy adds the detail and the process.

## 2. Conflicts of Interest

### 2.1 What a conflict is

A conflict of interest exists when your personal interests could affect, or appear to affect, your judgment at work (POL-012 section 6). Having a conflict is not wrongdoing. Hiding one is.

### 2.2 How to declare

Declare a conflict as soon as you become aware of it, using the Conflict of Interest Declaration form in the HR portal. The form goes to your manager and to People Operations. They reply within 10 business days with one of three outcomes: no action needed, conditions that manage the conflict, or a decision that the activity cannot continue alongside your role.

### 2.3 Annual confirmation

Every January, each employee confirms in the HR portal that their declarations are up to date. Employees at job level L6 and above, and everyone in Finance and Procurement, must complete the confirmation even if they have nothing to declare.

### 2.4 Common cases

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| A family member or partner works for a supplier, customer or competitor | Declare it. You take no part in decisions about that organization |
| You hold more than 1 percent of a supplier, customer or competitor | Declare it |
| You hold shares through a pension or an index fund | No declaration needed |
| A family member or partner applies to Fernhill | Declare it and take no part in the hiring (POL-021 section 3.3) |
| A romantic relationship with someone in your reporting line | Declare it to People Operations so the reporting line can be changed (POL-012 section 6) |
| You are offered a gift or hospitality | Follow the limits in POL-012 section 7 |

## 3. Outside Work

### 3.1 Approval

Paid work for another organization, and running a business of your own, need your manager's written approval before you start (POL-012 section 6). Request it with the Conflict of Interest Declaration form.

### 3.2 Conditions

Outside work is approved only if all of the following are true:

- It does not use Fernhill working time, equipment, systems or information. A company laptop may never be used for outside work (POL-008 section 6.3 forbids the reverse: personal computers may not be used for Fernhill work).
- It is not for a competitor of Fernhill, or for a customer or supplier that you deal with in your role.
- It does not affect your performance, your availability during core hours or your rest (POL-017 section 4).
- For a full-time employee, it takes no more than 10 hours per week on average. More than this needs the approval of your department director.

### 3.3 Part-time employees and interns

Part-time employees and interns may hold another job without the 10 hour limit. They must still declare it, and the other conditions in section 3.2 apply.

### 3.4 Review

Approval is reviewed in the annual confirmation. Fernhill may withdraw approval with 30 calendar days of written notice if the conditions are no longer met.

### 3.5 Outside work during leave

You may not do paid outside work while you are on sick leave, medical leave or paid parental leave from Fernhill, unless People Operations has agreed in writing.

## 4. Board Seats, Teaching and Volunteering

### 4.1 Unpaid roles

You do not need approval to volunteer, to coach a sports team, to serve on a school board or to hold an unpaid role in a charity or community organization. Declare the role only if the organization is a customer, supplier or competitor of Fernhill.

### 4.2 Board seats

A seat on the board of a for-profit company needs the approval of the Chief People Officer and Legal. Any fees are yours to keep.

### 4.3 Teaching and writing

Teaching a course, writing a book or writing articles for payment is outside work under section 3. If the subject is your work at Fernhill, section 7 also applies.

### 4.4 Volunteer days

Full-time and part-time employees who have completed the probationary period may take up to 2 paid volunteer days per calendar year to volunteer for a registered charity or community organization. Give your manager at least 5 business days of notice and record the day in the HR portal. Volunteer days are separate from paid time off. They do not carry over and are not paid out when employment ends. Political campaigning does not qualify.

## 5. Personal Projects and Intellectual Property

### 5.1 What Fernhill owns

Fernhill owns work you create as part of your job, and work you create using Fernhill time, equipment or Confidential information.

### 5.2 What you own

You own work that you create entirely in your own time, on your own equipment, without Fernhill information, and that does not relate to Fernhill's business or its planned products.

### 5.3 If you are not sure

If a personal project might relate to Fernhill's business, declare it with the Conflict of Interest Declaration form before you publish or sell it. Legal replies within 15 business days.

### 5.4 Open-source software

You may contribute to open-source projects in your own time under section 5.2. Contributing Fernhill code to an open-source project, or contributing during working time, needs the approval of your department director and IT Security.

## 6. Speaking About Fernhill in Public

### 6.1 Who speaks for Fernhill

Only the Communications team and people they have authorized may speak on behalf of Fernhill. If a journalist, analyst or blogger contacts you, do not answer their questions. Pass the request to communications@fernhill.example on the same day.

### 6.2 Customers and partners

Do not name a customer, show a customer logo or describe a customer's project in public unless Communications confirms that the customer has agreed in writing.

### 6.3 Legal matters and authorities

Requests from lawyers, courts, regulators or the police go to Legal on the same day (POL-020 section 7.3).

## 7. External Talks, Panels and Publications

### 7.1 Approval

A conference talk, panel, podcast, article or blog post about your work at Fernhill needs approval from your manager and from Communications. Submit the outline at least 10 business days before any submission deadline. Slides and text are reviewed again before the event to check that no Confidential or Restricted information is included.

### 7.2 Time and cost

If Fernhill has asked you to speak, the event is working time, travel is business travel under POL-007 section 4, and the conference does not count toward the limit of 2 conferences per year in POL-016 section 4.1.

### 7.3 Fees and gifts

A speaker fee for a talk given as part of your job belongs to Fernhill and is declined or passed to Finance. A speaker gift worth up to USD 100 may be kept, in line with the gift limit in POL-012 section 7.

### 7.4 Talks in a personal capacity

A talk on a subject that has nothing to do with Fernhill needs no approval. Do not use Fernhill branding, and give it in your own time or as PTO.

## 8. Social Media

### 8.1 Personal accounts

You are free to use social media. You may say that you work at Fernhill and describe your role. When you post about Fernhill, its products or its industry, make clear that the views are your own.

### 8.2 What not to post

- Confidential or Restricted information, including unreleased products, financial results, customer names and security details (POL-006 section 3).
- Photos of screens, whiteboards, building passes or office interiors that show information.
- Anything about a colleague that would breach POL-012, including harassment or bullying online.
- Personal information about colleagues (POL-020 section 14).
- Your travel plans together with the fact that you are carrying a company laptop.

### 8.3 Job postings and good news

You are encouraged to share Fernhill job postings and public announcements. Share the official post and do not add details that have not been published.

### 8.4 Reviews and endorsements

Do not post a review of a Fernhill product, a competitor's product or Fernhill as an employer without saying that you work at Fernhill. Do not endorse a supplier's product in a way that suggests Fernhill endorses it.

### 8.5 Complaints about work

This policy does not stop you from discussing your pay or your working conditions with others (POL-014 section 11). If you have a concern about how you are treated, the channels in POL-012 section 4 are more likely to resolve it than a public post.

## 9. Political and Civic Activity

You may take part in political and civic life in your own time. Do not use Fernhill's name, time, equipment or mailing lists for political activity, and do not suggest that Fernhill supports a party or candidate. Fernhill does not make political donations. Paid time to vote and jury duty leave are covered by POL-010 section 6.

## 10. Breaches

A failure to declare a conflict, outside work without approval, and disclosure of Confidential or Restricted information are breaches of this policy and may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including the end of employment (POL-012 section 10).

## 11. What the HR Assistant Can and Cannot Do

The HR assistant can explain these rules. It cannot approve outside work, decide whether something is a conflict of interest or review a talk or a post. It does not give legal advice about who owns a personal project.

## 12. Questions

Raise a ticket under "Policy Question". For anything about a possible conflict of interest that you would prefer not to put in a ticket, contact People Operations directly at people@fernhill.example.
