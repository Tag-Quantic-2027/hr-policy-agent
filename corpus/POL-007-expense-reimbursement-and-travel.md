# Expense Reimbursement and Travel Policy

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Fernhill reimburses employees for reasonable costs that are necessary for their work. This policy explains what can be claimed, the limits that apply, who approves a claim and how to submit one.

This policy applies to full-time employees, part-time employees and interns. Independent contractors are reimbursed only as their contract provides.

All amounts are in United States dollars (USD). Employees in Canada and Portugal claim in local currency. Finance converts the limits using the company exchange rate published on the first business day of each month.

## 2. General Rules

### 2.1 What makes an expense reimbursable

An expense is reimbursable only if it is:

- Incurred for a genuine business purpose.
- Reasonable in amount.
- Within the limits in this policy.
- Supported by a receipt where one is required.
- Approved by the right person (see section 3).

### 2.2 Receipts

An itemized receipt is required for every expense of USD 25 or more. A card statement is not a receipt. If a receipt is lost, you may submit a missing receipt declaration, up to 2 times per calendar year.

### 2.3 Deadline

Submit expenses within 30 calendar days of the date of the expense. Expenses submitted between 31 and 60 calendar days after the date need approval from your department director. Expenses submitted more than 60 calendar days after the date are not reimbursed.

### 2.4 Payment

Approved expenses are paid with the next payroll run after approval, provided they are approved at least 5 business days before the pay date.

## 3. Approval Limits

| Total value of the claim | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Up to USD 1,000 | Manager |
| More than USD 1,000 and up to USD 5,000 | Manager and department director |
| More than USD 5,000 | Manager, department director and a vice president |

A person can never approve their own expenses. Splitting one expense across several claims to stay under a limit is a breach of this policy.

## 4. Business Travel

Business travel is travel at Fernhill's request or with your manager's agreement for a business purpose. Travel must be approved by your manager before it is booked, and booked through the company travel tool where possible.

### 4.1 Flights

- Flights of less than 6 hours of scheduled flying time: economy class.
- Flights of 6 hours or more of scheduled flying time: premium economy is permitted.
- Business class is not reimbursed unless approved in advance by a vice president for a medical reason.
- Book at least 14 calendar days ahead where possible.

### 4.2 Hotels

The limit is USD 250 per night before tax. In high-cost cities the limit is USD 350 per night before tax. The high-cost cities are New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich, Tokyo and Singapore.

### 4.3 Meals while traveling

- Travel within your country of employment: up to USD 75 per day.
- International travel: up to USD 100 per day.

These are limits on actual spending, not allowances. Claim what you actually spent. Alcohol is reimbursed only as part of a meal and only up to USD 20 per person per day.

### 4.4 Ground transport

Use public transport, ride-share services or standard taxis. Rental cars need manager approval in advance and must be mid-size or smaller. Use of a personal car is reimbursed at USD 0.70 per mile (USD 0.44 per kilometer).

### 4.5 Combining business and personal travel

You may extend a business trip for personal reasons with your manager's approval. Fernhill pays only the costs that would have been incurred for the business trip alone. Working during a personal extension in another country counts toward the Work From Anywhere limit in POL-005.

## 5. Client and Team Expenses

### 5.1 Client meals and entertainment

Client meals are limited to USD 120 per person, including tax and tip. Record the names of the people present and the business purpose. Gifts to clients are limited to USD 100 per person per year and must comply with the gifts rules in POL-012 section 7.

### 5.2 Team events

Team meals and events are limited to USD 60 per person per event and need manager approval in advance. Each team has a budget for 4 events per year.

## 6. Home Office and Remote Work Costs

### 6.1 Home office stipend

Hybrid and fully remote employees may claim a one-time home office stipend of up to USD 500. The stipend covers furniture and accessories for a home workspace, such as a desk, an office chair, a monitor arm, a lamp or a footrest.

Rules for the home office stipend:

- It is available only after the probationary period is complete.
- It is a one-time amount for the whole of your employment, not an annual amount.
- Receipts are required for every item.
- It may be claimed in up to 3 separate claims within 12 months of the first claim.
- Office-based employees, interns and independent contractors are not eligible.
- Items bought with the stipend belong to you and do not need to be returned.

### 6.2 Monthly internet allowance

Fully remote employees receive an internet allowance of USD 50 per month, paid through payroll. No receipt is needed. Hybrid and office-based employees are not eligible.

### 6.3 Laptops and computer equipment

Laptops, desktop computers, tablets and phones are never reimbursable. Fernhill provides a company laptop and standard accessories to every employee under the Equipment and IT Asset Policy (POL-008). If you need different or additional computer equipment, request it from IT under POL-008. Do not buy it yourself and claim it.

Monitors, keyboards, mice, headsets and docking stations are provided by IT under POL-008 section 3 and should not be claimed against the home office stipend unless IT has confirmed in writing that it cannot supply the item.

### 6.4 Ergonomic equipment

If you need specialist ergonomic equipment for a medical reason, do not use the stipend. Raise an accommodation request with People Operations under POL-013. Approved accommodation equipment is paid for by Fernhill in full and does not count against the stipend.

### 6.5 Working from another location

The costs of choosing to work from another location under POL-005, including travel, accommodation, co-working space and internet access, are personal costs and are not reimbursable.

## 7. Professional Development

Courses, conferences, books and certifications are paid from the annual learning budget described in the Benefits Overview and Eligibility Policy (POL-009) section 7. Claim them through the expense tool under the category "Learning". Travel to an approved conference is business travel and is claimed under section 4.

## 8. Expenses That Are Not Reimbursable

The following are never reimbursed:

- Laptops, computers, tablets and phones (see section 6.3).
- Commuting between home and your assigned office.
- Fines and penalties, including parking and traffic fines.
- Personal entertainment, including in-room films and minibar items.
- Airline lounge memberships, seat upgrades and travel insurance bought personally.
- Clothing, luggage and personal care items.
- Costs for a partner or family member who travels with you.
- Home utilities other than the monthly internet allowance.
- Childcare and pet care.
- Costs of working from another location by personal choice (see section 6.5).

## 9. Company Credit Cards

Employees who travel more than 4 times per year may apply for a company credit card. A company card may be used only for business expenses. Every card transaction must be matched to a receipt in the expense tool within 30 calendar days. Personal use of a company card must be repaid immediately and reported to Finance.

## 10. Incorrect and Dishonest Claims

An honest mistake is corrected and is not penalized. Deliberately submitting a false or inflated claim is dishonesty and is handled under the Workplace Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy (POL-012). It may lead to the end of employment.

## 11. Questions

Raise a ticket in the HR portal under the category "Expense Question". Finance answers expense questions. See POL-013 for response times.
