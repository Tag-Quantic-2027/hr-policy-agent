Company Holidays and Office Closures

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy lists the paid company holidays for 2027 in each country of employment, explains floating holidays, and describes the winter office closure. It applies to full-time employees, part-time employees and interns. Independent contractors do not receive paid company holidays (see POL-001 section 3.4).

Company holidays are separate from paid time off. A company holiday is never deducted from your PTO balance, even if it falls during a period of approved PTO (see POL-002 section 8.2).

2. Which Holiday Calendar Applies to You

Your holiday calendar is set by your country of employment, as recorded in your employee profile. It is not set by the office you visit or by the place you are working from on a given day. An employee whose country of employment is Canada follows the Canada calendar even while working temporarily from another country under POL-005.

Part-time employees are paid for a company holiday only if it falls on a day they are normally scheduled to work.

6. Floating Holidays

6.1 Entitlement

In addition to the calendar for their country, every full-time and part-time employee receives 2 floating holidays per calendar year. Floating holidays let you observe a religious, cultural or personal day that is not on your country's calendar. Interns receive 1 floating holiday per internship term.

6.2 Rules

  • Floating holidays are available from your first day of employment. They are not subject to the probationary period restriction on PTO.
  • Request a floating holiday through the HR portal with at least 3 business days of notice. Your manager approves it.
  • Floating holidays do not carry over. Unused floating holidays expire on December 31.
  • Floating holidays are not paid out at the end of employment.
  • Employees who start on or after July 1 receive 1 floating holiday for that calendar year.

7. Winter Office Closure

All Fernhill offices are closed from Monday, December 27, 2027 to Friday, December 31, 2027. Full-time employees, part-time employees and interns are paid for their normally scheduled days during the closure. These 5 days are not deducted from PTO.

A small number of employees in Customer Success and IT Security are scheduled for on-call cover during the closure. Employees who work on-call days during the closure receive an equal number of days off, to be taken by March 31 of the following year, with manager approval.

The PTO blackout periods in POL-002 section 6 do not affect the winter office closure.

8. Working on a Company Holiday

Employees are not expected to work on a company holiday. If a manager asks an employee to work on a company holiday because of an urgent business need, and the employee agrees, the employee receives another day off within 30 calendar days. Employees who are eligible for overtime under local law are paid for holiday work as local law requires. People Operations answers holiday pay questions individually.

9. Holidays While Working From Another Location

If you are working temporarily from another country or region under POL-005, you continue to follow the holiday calendar for your country of employment. A public holiday in the place you are visiting is a normal working day for you unless you use PTO or a floating holiday.
