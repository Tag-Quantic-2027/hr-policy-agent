Business Travel Booking and Traveler Safety Procedure

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

1. Purpose and Scope

This procedure explains how to plan, approve and book a business trip, and how Fernhill looks after people while they travel. It applies to full-time employees, part-time employees and interns who travel for Fernhill. Independent contractors travel under the terms of their contract.

The spending limits for flights, hotels, meals and ground transport are set in the Expense Reimbursement and Travel Policy (POL-007) section 4. This procedure does not change those limits. It repeats them in section 4 for convenience. If the two documents ever appear to disagree about an amount, follow the order of precedence in POL-001 section 2.2 and tell People Operations.

This procedure is about travel at Fernhill's request. Working from another place by your own choice is covered by the Working From Another Location Policy (POL-005).

3. Before You Book

3.1 Ask whether the trip is needed

Consider a video meeting first. Travel for a purely internal meeting of fewer than 4 hours is not normally approved.

3.2 Manager approval

Every trip must be approved by your manager before it is booked (POL-007 section 4). Create a trip request in the company travel tool with the purpose, dates, destination and estimated cost. Your manager approves it in the tool.

3.3 Additional approval

  • A trip with an estimated total cost of more than USD 5,000 per traveler also needs approval from the department director.
  • International travel also needs approval from the department director, whatever the cost.
  • Travel to a country rated high risk by Fernhill's travel safety provider needs approval from a vice president and a safety briefing (section 7.2).
  • Business travel to a country on the Restricted Locations List is never approved (POL-006 section 7).

These are approvals to travel. Approval of the expense claim afterward follows the limits in POL-007 section 3.

3.4 Right to enter and work

You are responsible for holding a valid passport. Workplace Services checks whether a business visa is needed and Fernhill pays for it. Allow at least 30 calendar days for a trip that needs a visa. A business visit usually allows meetings and training but not productive work for a local customer. If you are unsure what your visit allows, ask Workplace Services before you book. The HR assistant does not advise on visas or work authorization (POL-013 section 5.2).

4. Booking

4.1 Use the travel tool

Book flights, trains, hotels and rental cars through the company travel tool where possible (POL-007 section 4). Bookings made in the tool are paid centrally, so you do not need to claim them, and they let Fernhill find you in an emergency. If you book outside the tool, enter the itinerary in the tool on the same day.

4.2 Flights

  • Flights of less than 6 hours of scheduled flying time: economy class.
  • Flights of 6 hours or more of scheduled flying time: premium economy is permitted.
  • Business class is not reimbursed unless approved in advance by a vice president for a medical reason.
  • Book at least 14 calendar days ahead where possible.

Choose the lowest logical fare: the cheapest fare that departs within 2 hours of your preferred time with no more than one connection. You may keep airline loyalty points earned on business travel. You may not choose a more expensive flight to earn them. Upgrades paid for with your own points or money are allowed.

4.3 Trains

For journeys of less than 4 hours, rail is preferred to flying where a direct service exists. Standard class applies for journeys of less than 3 hours and first class is permitted for longer journeys.

4.4 Hotels

The limit is USD 250 per night before tax, or USD 350 per night before tax in the high-cost cities: New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich, Tokyo and Singapore (POL-007 section 4.2). If no suitable hotel is available within the limit, for example during a large conference, ask your department director to approve a higher rate before you book. Private home rentals may be booked only through the travel tool.

4.5 Rental cars and personal cars

Rental cars need manager approval in advance and must be mid-size or smaller. Use of a personal car is reimbursed at USD 0.70 per mile (USD 0.44 per kilometer) (POL-007 section 4.4). Accept the insurance included in Fernhill's corporate rental rate and decline extra insurance. Do not drive for more than 2 hours after a flight of 6 hours or more.

4.6 Meals

Meals are reimbursed up to USD 75 per day for travel within your country of employment and USD 100 per day for international travel. These are limits on actual spending, not allowances (POL-007 section 4.3).

5. Changes and Cancellations

Change or cancel in the travel tool as soon as plans change. Fernhill pays change fees caused by a business reason, illness or a family emergency. Fees caused by a personal preference are paid by the traveler. Unused tickets are held as credit in the tool and must be used for your next trip on that airline.

If a flight is delayed or cancelled, call the travel tool's 24 hour support line, which can rebook you. If you are stranded overnight, you may book a hotel within the normal limit without further approval.

6. Travel Time, Working Hours and Time Off

  • Travel on a normal working day counts as working time. Travel of more than 4 hours on a weekend or company holiday is repaid with equal time off within 30 calendar days (POL-017 section 9).
  • After a flight of 8 hours or more that lands in the morning, you are not expected to work until the following day.
  • You follow the holiday calendar of your country of employment while traveling (POL-003 section 9).
  • Personal days added to a trip are PTO and need approval in the normal way (POL-002 section 3). Working during a personal extension in another country counts toward the Work From Anywhere limit (POL-007 section 4.5).

7. Traveler Safety

7.1 Travel insurance and medical help

Everyone traveling for Fernhill is covered by the company business travel insurance, which includes emergency medical treatment and evacuation. The emergency assistance number is on the insurance card in the travel tool and is answered 24 hours a day. This insurance covers business travel only. It does not cover a personal extension or time spent working abroad under POL-005 (POL-009 section 9.3).

7.2 Risk ratings

The travel tool shows a risk rating for each destination: low, medium or high. For a high risk destination you must complete a safety briefing with Workplace Services at least 5 business days before departure and share a daily check-in time. You may decline to travel to a high risk destination without giving a reason, and declining must not affect your review.

7.3 If something goes wrong

  1. In immediate danger, call the local emergency number.
  2. Call the emergency assistance number on the insurance card.
  3. Tell your manager when it is safe to do so.

After a major incident in a place where employees are traveling, Workplace Services contacts each traveler in the area. Reply as soon as you can.

7.4 Health

Fernhill pays for vaccinations and medication recommended for a business destination. If a health condition affects your ability to travel, speak to People Operations in confidence. You do not need to tell your manager the reason (POL-019 section 5).

7.5 Traveling alone and at night

Use licensed taxis or ride-share services. If you arrive after 22:00, you may take a taxi instead of public transport, whatever the cost difference.

8. Devices and Data While Traveling

  • Carry your laptop in hand luggage. Never check it into the hold (POL-008 section 5).
  • Use the VPN on every network outside a Fernhill office (POL-006 section 6.1).
  • Restricted data may not be accessed from outside your country of employment, including on a business trip (POL-006 section 3.4). Employees with a Restricted data access level may travel abroad for business only if their manager confirms that the trip does not need that access.
  • Report a lost or stolen device within 1 hour of noticing it (POL-006 section 8.2).
  • If a border official asks you to unlock a device, comply, and report it to IT Security as soon as you can.

9. Traveling With Others

A partner or family member may accompany you at your own cost. Fernhill pays only what the trip would have cost for you alone. No more than 6 members of the same team, and no more than 2 members of the executive team, should travel on the same flight.

10. After the Trip

Submit any expenses not paid through the travel tool within 30 calendar days of the date of the expense, with itemized receipts for every expense of USD 25 or more (POL-007 sections 2.2 and 2.3).

11. What the HR Assistant Can and Cannot Do

The HR assistant can explain these rules and the limits in POL-007. It cannot book travel, approve a trip or approve an expense claim.

12. Questions

Questions about booking go to Workplace Services through the travel tool. Questions about reimbursement may be raised as a ticket under "Expense Question". See POL-013 for response times.
