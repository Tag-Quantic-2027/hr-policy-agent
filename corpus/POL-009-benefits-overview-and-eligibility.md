# Benefits Overview and Eligibility

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document describes the benefits Fernhill Technologies offers, who is eligible for each and when eligibility begins. It is a summary. The plan documents issued by each benefits provider contain the full terms, and if this summary and a plan document disagree, the plan document applies.

Eligibility depends on your employment classification (POL-001 section 3) and your country of employment. Your current elections are shown in the HR portal.

## 2. Eligibility at a Glance

| Benefit | Full-time | Part-time, 20 or more hours per week | Part-time, fewer than 20 hours per week | Intern | Contractor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Health plan | Yes | Yes, at a reduced employer contribution | No | No | No |
| Dental and vision | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |
| Retirement plan with employer match | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Wellness allowance | Yes | Yes, prorated | No | No | No |
| Learning budget | Yes | Yes, prorated | No | No | No |
| Employee Assistance Program | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Life and disability insurance | Yes | No | No | No | No |

Independent contractors are not eligible for any Fernhill benefit.

## 3. Health Plan

### 3.1 When coverage starts

Health coverage starts on the first day of the month following your start date. An employee who starts on January 11 is covered from February 1. An employee who starts on the first day of a month is covered from that day. Coverage does not depend on completing the probationary period.

### 3.2 Plans

Two plans are offered in the United States:

- Core plan: lower monthly cost to the employee, with a higher deductible.
- Plus plan: higher monthly cost to the employee, with a lower deductible and a wider network.

If you do not make an election within 30 calendar days of your start date, you are enrolled in the Core plan with employee-only coverage.

### 3.3 Employer contribution

For full-time employees, Fernhill pays 90 percent of the monthly cost of employee-only coverage on the Core plan and 80 percent on the Plus plan. Fernhill pays 60 percent of the additional cost of covering dependents on either plan.

For part-time employees scheduled for 20 or more hours per week, Fernhill pays 50 percent of the monthly cost of employee-only coverage on either plan and does not contribute to the cost of dependents.

### 3.4 Dependents

You may cover a spouse or domestic partner and children up to the age of 26. Proof of the relationship is required within 30 calendar days of enrollment.

## 4. Dental and Vision

Dental and vision coverage starts on the same date as health coverage. Fernhill pays 100 percent of the cost of employee-only dental and vision coverage for full-time employees and 50 percent for eligible part-time employees. Dependents may be added at the employee's cost.

## 5. Retirement Plan

### 5.1 Your contributions

You may contribute to the retirement plan from your first pay period.

### 5.2 Employer match

Fernhill matches your contributions up to 4 percent of your base pay. The employer match starts after you have completed 90 calendar days of employment. Contributions you make during the first 90 days are not matched afterward.

### 5.3 Vesting

Employer match contributions vest over 3 years: one third after each completed year of service. Your own contributions are always fully yours.

## 6. Wellness Allowance

Eligible employees may claim up to USD 600 per calendar year for wellness costs such as gym memberships, fitness classes, sports equipment and mental health apps. The allowance is prorated for part-time employees by scheduled hours and for employees who join during the year by the number of full months remaining. Claim through the expense tool under the category "Wellness" with receipts. Unused allowance does not carry over.

## 7. Learning Budget

### 7.1 Amount and eligibility

Eligible employees have a learning budget of USD 1,500 per calendar year for courses, conferences, books and certifications related to their current role or a role they could reasonably move into at Fernhill. The learning budget becomes available after 6 months of employment.

### 7.2 Approval

Spending of up to USD 500 on a single item needs manager approval. A single item costing more than USD 500 needs approval from the department director as well. Claims follow the Expense Reimbursement and Travel Policy (POL-007) section 7.

### 7.3 Repayment

If you leave Fernhill voluntarily within 6 months of completing a course or certification that cost more than USD 1,000, you may be asked to repay half of the cost, where local law allows.

### 7.4 Carryover

Unused learning budget does not carry over to the next calendar year.

## 8. Other Benefits

### 8.1 Employee Assistance Program

The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers confidential counseling and practical advice, 24 hours a day. It is available to full-time employees, part-time employees and interns, and to members of their household, from the first day of employment. Each person may use up to 8 counseling sessions per issue per year at no cost. Fernhill is not told who uses the EAP.

### 8.2 Life and disability insurance

Full-time employees are covered by life insurance of 2 times base pay and by long-term disability insurance, both paid for by Fernhill, from the first day of the month following the start date.

### 8.3 Commuter benefit

United States employees with an office-based or hybrid work arrangement may set aside pre-tax pay for public transport costs. Fernhill does not contribute to commuting costs (POL-007 section 8).

## 9. Benefits by Country and While Abroad

### 9.1 Canada

Employees in Canada are covered by their provincial health plan. Fernhill provides a supplementary health plan covering prescriptions, dental, vision and paramedical services, with the same start date and employer contribution rules as section 3. The retirement plan is a group registered retirement savings plan with the same 4 percent match.

### 9.2 Portugal

Employees in Portugal are covered by the national health service. Fernhill provides private health insurance for the employee, fully paid by Fernhill, with dependents covered at 50 percent. A meal allowance is paid for each working day as required by the local agreement. The 4 percent retirement match is paid into a private pension plan.

### 9.3 Coverage while working from another location

Your health plan is designed for use in your country of employment. Cover outside that country is usually limited to emergencies and may not apply at all. Before working from another country under the Working From Another Location Policy (POL-005), check your plan documents and arrange travel medical insurance if you need it. The cost of travel medical insurance for personal travel or Work From Anywhere is not reimbursable (POL-007 section 8).

## 10. Changing Your Elections

### 10.1 Open enrollment

Open enrollment runs from November 1 to November 15 each year. Changes made during open enrollment take effect on January 1.

### 10.2 Qualifying life events

Outside open enrollment, you may change your elections only after a qualifying life event: marriage or divorce, the birth or adoption of a child, the death of a dependent, or a dependent losing other coverage. You must request the change within 30 calendar days of the event, with supporting documents. Raise a ticket in the HR portal under the category "Benefits Question".

### 10.3 Changes in classification

If your classification or scheduled hours change, your eligibility is reassessed from the effective date of the change. People Operations will write to you about any benefit that starts or ends.

## 11. When Benefits End

Health, dental and vision coverage ends on the last day of the month in which employment ends. Information about continuing coverage at your own cost is sent with your final pay details. The wellness allowance and learning budget end on your last day. Claims must be submitted before your last day.

## 12. What the HR Assistant Can and Cannot Do

The HR assistant can explain these rules and show you your own current elections. It cannot change an election, enroll a dependent, or tell you whether a specific medical treatment is covered. Questions about whether a treatment is covered go to the plan provider. Questions that involve medical information are handled only by People Operations staff (see POL-013 section 5).

## 13. Questions

Raise a ticket in the HR portal under the category "Benefits Question". See POL-013 for response times.
