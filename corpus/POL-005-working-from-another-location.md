# Working From Another Location Policy

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Employees sometimes want to work for a short time from somewhere other than their home or assigned office, for example to visit family or extend a trip. This policy, also known as the Work From Anywhere program, sets out when that is allowed, for how long, and what approval is needed.

Working from another location creates real obligations for Fernhill. Depending on where you are and for how long, it can create tax, payroll, immigration and data security obligations for both you and the company. The limits in this policy exist to keep those risks under control.

This policy covers temporary working from another location. A permanent move is covered by the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy (POL-004) section 6. Business travel at Fernhill's request is covered by the Expense Reimbursement and Travel Policy (POL-007) and does not count toward the limits in this policy.

## 2. Key Definitions

- **Country of employment:** the country where Fernhill employs you and runs your payroll, as recorded in your employee profile.
- **Home location:** the state or province where you live, as recorded in your employee profile.
- **Domestic remote work:** working temporarily from a different state or province within your country of employment.
- **International remote work:** working temporarily from any country other than your country of employment.
- **Working day:** a day on which you perform any work for Fernhill. PTO days, company holidays and weekends on which you do not work are not working days.

## 3. Eligibility

You are eligible for the Work From Anywhere program if all of the following are true:

- You are a full-time or part-time employee. Interns and independent contractors are not eligible.
- You have completed the probationary period described in POL-001 section 4.
- Your work arrangement is hybrid or fully remote. Office-based employees are not eligible.
- You are not on a performance improvement plan.
- Your data access level allows it. See section 6.

## 4. Domestic Remote Work

### 4.1 Limit

You may work from a different state or province within your country of employment for up to 30 consecutive calendar days at a time, and no more than 60 calendar days in total in a calendar year.

### 4.2 Approval and notice

Domestic remote work needs approval from your manager. Give at least 5 business days of notice. No ticket is needed for domestic remote work of 30 consecutive calendar days or fewer, but you must record the dates and location in the HR portal.

### 4.3 Longer domestic stays

Working from a different state or province for more than 30 consecutive calendar days can create payroll tax obligations in that state or province. A longer stay needs a payroll and tax review by People Operations. Raise a ticket under the category "Work From Anywhere" at least 15 business days before the stay begins. If the state or province is not on the registered list in POL-004 section 6.2, the request is normally declined.

## 5. International Remote Work

### 5.1 Limit

You may work from a country other than your country of employment for up to 20 working days in a calendar year. The 20 working days may be taken in one block or several.

### 5.2 Approval and notice

International remote work always needs both of the following before you travel:

1. Approval from your manager.
2. Approval from People Operations, requested through a ticket in the category "Work From Anywhere" at least 15 business days before your departure.

The ticket must state the country, the dates, the number of working days, and confirmation that you have the legal right to work from that country (see section 5.5).

### 5.3 Requests above the limit

A request to work internationally for more than 20 working days in a calendar year is an exception request. Exception requests need approval from your department director and from the Chief People Officer, following a tax and legal review. Raise the ticket at least 30 business days before departure. Exceptions are rare. They are normally approved only for serious family circumstances and are never approved for more than 40 working days in a calendar year.

If an exception is not approved, the usual alternatives are to work the first 20 working days and take PTO or unpaid personal leave (POL-010 section 7) for the remainder, or to shorten the stay.

### 5.4 Countries where work is not permitted

You may not work from a country on the Restricted Locations List in the Information and Data Security Policy (POL-006) section 7, for any length of time. No exception is available.

### 5.5 Your right to work in the other country

You are responsible for making sure that you are legally allowed to work from the country you are visiting. A tourist visa or visa-free entry does not always allow remote work. Fernhill does not sponsor visas for the Work From Anywhere program and does not give immigration advice. If you are unsure, check with the authorities of the country you plan to visit before you raise your ticket.

### 5.6 Tax

For stays within the 20 working day limit, Fernhill continues to run your payroll in your country of employment as normal. You are responsible for any personal tax obligation that arises from your own circumstances, such as citizenship or prior time spent in the other country. Fernhill does not give personal tax advice.

## 6. Data Access Restrictions

Your data access level is recorded in your employee profile and is defined in POL-006 section 3.

| Data access level | Domestic remote work | International remote work |
|---|---|---|
| Internal | Permitted | Permitted |
| Confidential | Permitted | Permitted, with the VPN and device rules in POL-006 section 6 |
| Restricted | Permitted | Not permitted |

Employees with Restricted data access, such as payroll, finance systems and security administration roles, may not perform international remote work. This is because Restricted data may not be accessed from outside the employee's country of employment (POL-006 section 3.4). The only alternative is for the department director to arrange temporary removal of Restricted access for the period, which is granted only if the employee's duties can be covered by a colleague.

## 7. While You Are Working From Another Location

- Follow every rule in POL-006, including the use of the VPN at all times and the rules on public networks.
- Use only your company laptop for work. Carry it in hand luggage. See POL-008 section 7 on taking equipment abroad.
- Keep the core hours in POL-001 section 5.2 unless your manager agrees otherwise in writing. Core hours are set in the time zone of your assigned office, not the place you are visiting.
- Follow the holiday calendar for your country of employment (POL-003 section 9).
- Report a lost or stolen device within 1 hour of noticing it (POL-006 section 8).

## 8. Costs

Working from another location is your personal choice. Fernhill does not pay for travel, accommodation, co-working space, internet access or any other cost of working from another location. These costs are not reimbursable under POL-007.

Your company health coverage may not apply outside your country of employment. Check your plan before you travel and arrange your own travel medical insurance if needed. See POL-009 section 9.

## 9. Counting Days

Only working days count toward the international limit. If you spend 6 weeks abroad, take 2 of those weeks as PTO and work the other 4 weeks, you have used 20 working days. Days of PTO do not count (POL-002 section 8.3). Company holidays on your own country's calendar do not count either.

People Operations keeps the official count. Your remaining Work From Anywhere days for the year are shown in the HR portal.

## 10. Breaches

Working from another location without the required approval, or working from a country on the Restricted Locations List, is a serious breach of policy and may lead to disciplinary action. If you find yourself unexpectedly unable to return, for example because of a cancelled flight, tell your manager and People Operations on the same day.

## 11. Questions

Raise a ticket in the HR portal under the category "Work From Anywhere". See POL-013 for response times.
