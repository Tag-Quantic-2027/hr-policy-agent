# Hiring, Referrals and Internal Mobility Policy

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains how Fernhill fills roles: how a role is opened, how candidates are assessed, how employees can refer people they know, how employees can move to another role inside Fernhill, how contractors are engaged, and how earlier service is treated when someone returns. It applies to everyone involved in hiring and to every employee who refers a candidate or applies for an internal role.

## 2. Opening a Role

### 2.1 Approval

A new role needs approval from the department director and from Finance. A replacement for someone who has left needs approval from the department director only. People Operations assigns the job level and pay band before the role is advertised (POL-014 section 2).

### 2.2 Internal posting first

Every role is posted on the internal job board for 5 business days before it is advertised externally. The only exceptions are confidential roles approved by the Chief People Officer and the conversion of an intern or contractor who is already doing the work.

### 2.3 Work arrangement and location

The posting states the work arrangement, the assigned office and the locations where the role can be based. A role can be based only where Fernhill can employ people: the registered states and provinces and Portugal (POL-004 section 6.2).

## 3. Assessing Candidates

### 3.1 Fair process

Every candidate for the same role is assessed against the same criteria, with the same interview stages. Interviewers record their assessment separately before discussing the candidate. Decisions are never based on any characteristic protected by law (POL-012 section 3.1).

### 3.2 Interview panel

Each candidate is interviewed by at least 2 people, at least one of whom is outside the hiring manager's team. Anyone who interviews must first complete the interviewer training in the Fernhill Learning Library.

### 3.3 Conflicts of interest

If you know a candidate personally, tell People Operations and take no part in assessing them. You may not hire, manage or decide the pay of a family member or partner (POL-012 section 6).

### 3.4 Accommodations for candidates

Candidates may ask for an accommodation at any stage (POL-019 section 1).

### 3.5 Offers

Only People Operations may make an offer of employment. A manager must not state or promise pay, a start date, a job level or a work arrangement before People Operations has issued the written offer. Offers depend on right to work verification and, for roles with Restricted data access, a background check where local law allows.

### 3.6 Candidate records

Records of unsuccessful candidates are kept for 12 months (POL-020 section 6).

## 4. Employee Referrals

### 4.1 How to refer

Submit the candidate through the referral form on the internal job board before the candidate applies in any other way. A referral made after the candidate has already applied does not count.

### 4.2 Referral bonus

| Role | Referral bonus |
|---|---|
| Standard roles | USD 2,000 |
| Roles marked "priority" on the job board | USD 4,000 |
| Internships | No bonus |

The bonus is paid through payroll, and is taxable, with the first pay date after the person you referred completes their probationary period (POL-001 section 4). You must still be employed by Fernhill, and not serving a notice period, on that date. If the probationary period is extended, the bonus is paid when the extended period is completed.

### 4.3 Who can receive a referral bonus

Full-time employees, part-time employees and interns may receive a referral bonus. The following may not:

- The hiring manager for the role, and anyone in the reporting line above the role.
- Anyone on the interview panel for the role.
- Members of People Operations.
- Directors and above.
- Independent contractors.

### 4.4 Other rules

- A referral remains valid for 6 months. If the person is hired into a different role within that time, the bonus is still paid.
- No bonus is paid for referring a former Fernhill employee who left less than 12 months ago, or for referring a current contractor or intern.
- If two employees refer the same person, the first referral received counts.
- A referred candidate receives no advantage in assessment.

## 5. Converting Interns and Contractors

### 5.1 Interns

An intern may be offered employment at the end of the internship without the role being posted. Waiting periods in other policies are measured from the original start date of the internship unless the policy says otherwise (POL-001 section 3.5). A new probationary period does not apply if the internship lasted 90 calendar days or more.

### 5.2 Contractors

A contractor who becomes an employee is a new hire. The start date is the first day of employment. Time spent as a contractor does not count toward the probationary period, PTO entitlement by tenure, benefit waiting periods or any other waiting period.

## 6. Internal Transfers

### 6.1 Eligibility

You may apply for a role on the internal job board if all of the following are true:

- You have been in your current role for at least 12 months.
- Your most recent performance rating is "Meets expectations" or higher, where a review exists (POL-015 section 4).
- You are not on a performance improvement plan (POL-015 section 6.3).
- You have completed the probationary period.

The department director of the hiring team may waive the 12 month rule with the agreement of your current director. No one may waive the other conditions.

### 6.2 Telling your manager

You do not need your manager's permission to apply. You must tell your manager before your first interview. The hiring manager must not contact your manager before you have done so.

### 6.3 Assessment

Internal candidates go through the same assessment as external candidates for the role, and receive feedback within 5 business days of a decision.

### 6.4 Moving to the new role

The move takes place within 30 calendar days of the offer being accepted. The two managers may agree up to 45 calendar days when a handover is complex. A current manager cannot block or delay a move beyond 45 calendar days.

### 6.5 What changes and what does not

- Pay follows the pay band of the new role and, where the level is higher, the rule in POL-014 section 5.5. An internal move to a higher level takes effect on the date of the move and does not wait for April 1 or October 1.
- The probationary period does not start again.
- Your start date, PTO entitlement, PTO balance and benefits do not change.
- Your data access level is set for the new role from the date of the move. If the new role has Restricted data access, the limits on international remote work in POL-005 section 6 apply from that date, including to requests already approved.
- Your work arrangement is the one stated for the new role. A change of home location follows POL-004 section 6.3, and a change of country follows POL-022.

## 7. Temporary Assignments

A temporary assignment to another team for up to 6 months needs the agreement of both managers and is recorded in the HR portal. Your role, manager of record, pay and benefits do not change. An assignment of more than 6 months is treated as an internal transfer.

## 8. Engaging Independent Contractors

### 8.1 When a contractor is appropriate

A contractor may be engaged for a defined piece of work or to cover a short-term need. A contractor must not be used to fill a permanent role.

### 8.2 Limits

A contractor engagement is agreed for up to 12 months. Any extension beyond 12 months needs a review by People Operations and Legal, which may require the role to be converted to employment or ended.

### 8.3 The Fernhill sponsor

Every contractor has a Fernhill sponsor, who is the manager responsible for the work. The sponsor is recorded as the contractor's manager in the HR portal for access purposes only. Contractors do not receive PTO, benefits, stipends or access to the Work From Anywhere program (POL-001 section 3.4), and the sponsor cannot grant them.

## 9. Returning to Fernhill

### 9.1 Rehire within 12 months

If a former employee is rehired within 12 months of their last day:

- The original start date is used to decide the annual PTO entitlement by tenure (POL-002 section 2.1) and vesting of the employer match (POL-009 section 5.3).
- The probationary period applies again in full, and with it the rules on PTO during probation (POL-002 section 5).
- Benefit waiting periods are measured from the new start date.

### 9.2 Rehire after more than 12 months

A former employee rehired more than 12 months after their last day is treated as a new hire for every purpose.

### 9.3 Record

The HR portal shows the new start date as the start date. The original start date is recorded separately as the service date.

## 10. What the HR Assistant Can and Cannot Do

The HR assistant can explain these rules and tell you whether you meet the conditions in section 6.1 that it can see in your own record. It cannot see performance ratings, so it cannot confirm that condition. It cannot submit a referral or an application for you, and it does not comment on candidates.

## 11. Questions

Raise a ticket under "Policy Question". Questions about a referral bonus that has not been paid go under "Payroll Question". See POL-013 for response times.
