# Onboarding and Probation Procedure

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This procedure sets out what happens before a new employee starts, during the first week and through the first 90 days. It tells new employees what to expect and tells managers what they are responsible for.

It applies to new full-time and part-time employees. A shorter version, described in section 9, applies to interns. Independent contractors are onboarded by their Fernhill sponsor and are not covered by this procedure, other than the security training in section 5.

## 2. Roles

- **New employee:** completes the tasks assigned to them on time and asks for help when something is unclear.
- **Manager:** owns the onboarding plan, sets goals and holds the check-ins in section 6.
- **Onboarding buddy:** a colleague outside the reporting line who answers everyday questions for the first 90 days.
- **People Operations:** runs orientation, verifies right to work documents and tracks completion.
- **IT Operations:** provides equipment and accounts.

## 3. Before the First Day

### 3.1 Offer accepted

Within 2 business days of an accepted offer, People Operations sends a welcome email with a link to the HR portal, where the new employee completes their personal details, bank details and tax forms.

### 3.2 Equipment

IT ships the standard equipment in the Equipment and IT Asset Policy (POL-008) section 2 so that it arrives at least 5 business days before the start date for hybrid and fully remote employees. Office-based employees collect their equipment on the first day. Additional equipment for a home workspace can be requested after the start date under POL-008 section 3.

### 3.3 Manager preparation

At least 5 business days before the start date, the manager:

- Writes a 30, 60 and 90 day plan with clear goals.
- Assigns an onboarding buddy.
- Books the first week of introductory meetings.
- Confirms the work arrangement and assigned office with People Operations.

## 4. The First Day and First Week

### 4.1 First day

- Orientation session with People Operations, held at 10:00 in the time zone of the assigned office on the start date.
- Laptop setup and account activation with IT.
- Meeting with the manager to walk through the 30, 60 and 90 day plan.
- Introduction to the onboarding buddy.

### 4.2 Right to work verification

The new employee must provide original documents proving their right to work in their country of employment within 3 business days of the start date. This is a legal requirement. An employee who has not provided the documents by the end of the third business day cannot continue working until they do.

### 4.3 First week checklist

By the end of the first week, the new employee should have:

- Completed Security Awareness training (see section 5).
- Set up multi-factor authentication and the password manager (POL-006 section 4).
- Read the Employee Handbook Overview (POL-001).
- Met every member of their immediate team.
- Confirmed that their personal details and emergency contact are correct in the HR portal.

## 5. Required Training

| Training | Deadline | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| Security Awareness | Within 5 business days of the start date | Everyone, including interns and contractors |
| Code of Conduct and Respectful Workplace | Within 14 calendar days of the start date | Employees and interns |
| Data Privacy Essentials | Within 30 calendar days of the start date | Employees and interns |
| Manager Essentials | Within 60 calendar days of becoming a manager | People managers |
| Handling Restricted Data | Before Restricted access is granted | Anyone with a Restricted data access level |

Access to Confidential systems is paused if Security Awareness training is not complete by the deadline. It is restored when the training is complete.

## 6. Check-ins at 30, 60 and 90 Days

### 6.1 The 30 day check-in

The manager and employee review progress against the 30 day goals, confirm that the employee has the tools and access they need, and discuss anything that is unclear. The manager records a short summary in the HR portal.

### 6.2 The 60 day check-in

The manager gives clear feedback on what is going well and what needs to improve. If there is a risk that the employee will not pass probation, the manager must say so at this check-in and must tell People Operations within 2 business days. From day 61, the employee may take accrued PTO with manager approval (POL-002 section 5).

### 6.3 The 90 day review

The manager completes the probation review in the HR portal no later than day 85, so that the outcome can be confirmed before day 90. There are three possible outcomes:

- **Probation passed.** The employee is confirmed in the role.
- **Probation extended.** Probation is extended once, by up to 30 calendar days, with written approval from People Operations (POL-001 section 4.3).
- **Employment ended.** This requires a review by People Operations before the employee is told.

## 8. Onboarding Checklist for Managers

A manager can ask the HR assistant to prepare an onboarding checklist for a new direct report. The checklist is built from this procedure and from the new employee's profile. It includes:

1. Before the start date: the plan, the buddy, the first week of meetings and confirmation of equipment delivery.
2. First day: orientation, laptop setup and the plan walkthrough.
3. First week: right to work verification by business day 3, Security Awareness training by business day 5.
4. Training deadlines calculated from the start date.
5. Dates of the 30, 60 and 90 day check-ins, and the day 85 deadline for the probation review.
6. Items specific to the employee, such as home workspace equipment for a fully remote employee, or Handling Restricted Data training for a role with Restricted access.

The checklist is a draft for the manager to review. The HR assistant does not book meetings or assign training.

## 9. Interns

Interns follow a shorter procedure: orientation and equipment on the first day, Security Awareness training within 5 business days, Code of Conduct training within 14 calendar days, a mid-term check-in at the halfway point and a final review in the last week. Interns do not have a probationary period.

## 10. Problems During Onboarding

If equipment has not arrived, accounts do not work or training cannot be accessed, the new employee should tell their manager and raise a ticket in the HR portal under the category "Onboarding". People Operations aims to resolve onboarding problems within 1 business day, because every day lost in the first week matters.

## 11. Questions

Raise a ticket in the HR portal under the category "Onboarding". See POL-013 for response times.
