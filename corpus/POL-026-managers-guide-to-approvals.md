# Manager's Guide to People Approvals

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

## 1. Purpose and Status of This Guide

This guide is for anyone who manages people at Fernhill. It collects, in one place, the decisions a manager is asked to make under the people policies, who else must approve, and how quickly a manager must respond.

This guide is a summary. It creates no rules of its own. Every row cites the policy that sets the rule, and that policy always takes priority over this guide, in the same way that a detailed policy takes priority over the Employee Handbook (POL-001 section 2.2). The fact that this guide has a later effective date than a policy it summarizes does not give it priority. If you find a difference between this guide and a policy, follow the policy and tell People Operations.

## 2. What a Manager Can and Cannot Decide

### 2.1 You can

- Approve or decline requests that a policy gives to "the manager".
- Agree informal flexibility in start and finish times (POL-017 section 3.1).
- Recommend, but not grant, an exception that a policy reserves for someone else.

### 2.2 You cannot

- Approve an exception that a policy reserves for a director, People Operations or the Chief People Officer (POL-013 section 6.3).
- Approve your own request or your own expenses (POL-007 section 3).
- Make or promise an offer of employment, a pay change or a promotion (POL-021 section 3.5).
- Start a performance improvement plan without People Operations (POL-015 section 6.2).
- End someone's employment (POL-018 section 10.1).
- Grant PTO, benefits, stipends or Work From Anywhere to a contractor you sponsor (POL-021 section 8.3).

### 2.3 If your direct report has no manager on file

If you are covering a team whose manager has left, you do not become the approver automatically. People Operations routes requests to the acting manager they have recorded (POL-002 section 3.5).

| PTO in a blackout period (Finance and Sales) | Manager and department director | As above | POL-002 section 6 |
| Floating holiday | Manager | 3 business days | POL-003 section 6 |

| Unpaid personal leave of more than 2 weeks | Manager, department director and a vice president | 30 calendar days | POL-010 section 7.2 |

You must respond to a PTO request within 3 business days (POL-002 section 3.4). A request is not approved until you approve it in the HR portal. Saying yes in a meeting is not approval.

A negative balance may never be approved during the probationary period, and may never go below minus 3 days.

Sick leave and bereavement leave are reported to you, not approved by you (POL-010 sections 2.3 and 5). You are told the dates of a leave. You are not told medical details and must not ask for them (POL-010 section 10).

## 4. Where People Work

| Request | Who approves | Lead time | Policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic remote work, up to 30 consecutive calendar days | Manager | 5 business days | POL-005 section 4.2 |
| Domestic remote work of more than 30 consecutive calendar days | Manager, after a payroll and tax review by People Operations | Ticket 15 business days before | POL-005 section 4.3 |
| International remote work, up to 20 working days per calendar year | Manager and People Operations | Ticket 15 business days before departure | POL-005 section 5.2 |
| International remote work above 20 working days | Department director and Chief People Officer | Ticket 30 business days before departure | POL-005 section 5.3 |
| Change between hybrid and fully remote | Manager and department director | 15 business days for a decision | POL-004 section 4.2 |
| Moving home to another state or province | People Operations, through a ticket | 30 calendar days before the move | POL-004 section 6.3 |
| Permanent move to another Fernhill country | Both department directors and the Chief People Officer | 90 calendar days | POL-022 section 6.1 |
| Compressed work week | Manager and department director | 3 month trial | POL-017 section 5.3 |

Before you approve any Work From Anywhere request, check the eligibility conditions in POL-005 section 3. You cannot approve a request from someone who is on probation, office-based, on a performance improvement plan, an intern or a contractor. You cannot approve international remote work for someone with a Restricted data access level (POL-005 section 6), and no one can approve work from a country on the Restricted Locations List (POL-006 section 7).

Your approval alone is never enough for international remote work. The employee must not travel until People Operations has also approved the ticket.

## 7. Things You Must Pass On

Some matters are not yours to resolve. Pass them on within the time shown.

| What you hear | What you do | Policy |
|---|---|---|
| A concern about harassment, discrimination, bullying or retaliation | Pass it to People Operations within 1 business day, even if the employee asks you not to act | POL-012 section 10 |
| A risk that a new employee will not pass probation | Tell People Operations within 2 business days of the 60 day check-in | POL-011 section 6.2 |
| A medical condition or a request for an adjustment | Do not record it. Direct the employee to an Accommodation Request | POL-019 section 5 |
| A lost device or a suspected security incident | Make sure it is reported at once. A lost device must be reported within 1 hour | POL-006 section 8.2 |
| A work-related injury | Make sure it is reported to People Operations within 24 hours | POL-019 section 8.3 |
| A suspected pay error | Direct the employee to a Payroll Question ticket | POL-014 section 8.1 |
| A request from a lender, lawyer or authority about an employee | Do not answer. Pass it to People Operations or Legal | POL-020 section 7 |

## 8. What You Can See About Your Team

You can see the following for your direct reports only: job title, department, employment classification, work arrangement, assigned office, start date, time off balances and time off requests (POL-013 section 7.2). You cannot see benefits elections, medical information, bank details or Workplace Concern tickets. The HR assistant applies the same limits when you are signed in. It will show you a direct report's PTO balance. It will not show you the balance of someone who does not report to you, and it will not show anyone your team's records except you and People Operations.

## 9. Using the HR Assistant as a Manager

The HR assistant can explain any policy and cite the section, check a rule against the record of the person signed in, and prepare a draft ticket or a draft message. It cannot approve anything. When an employee shows you an answer from the HR assistant, treat it as an explanation of policy. The approval is still yours to give or decline in the HR portal, and you remain responsible for it.
