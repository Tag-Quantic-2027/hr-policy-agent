# Learning and Development Policy

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy describes the support Fernhill gives for learning: the learning budget, study leave, conferences, tuition assistance, required training and mentoring. It applies to full-time and part-time employees. Section 8 explains what is available to interns. Independent contractors are not covered by this policy.

The amount of the learning budget and who is eligible for it are set in the Benefits Overview and Eligibility Policy (POL-009) section 7. This policy explains how to use it. If the two documents ever appear to disagree about the amount or the waiting period, follow the order of precedence in POL-001 section 2.2 and tell People Operations.

## 2. The Learning Budget in Practice

### 2.1 Summary of the rules in POL-009

- The learning budget is USD 1,500 per calendar year.
- It becomes available after 6 months of employment.
- It is prorated for part-time employees who work 20 or more hours per week. Part-time employees who work fewer than 20 hours per week are not eligible.
- A single item of up to USD 500 needs manager approval. A single item of more than USD 500 also needs approval from the department director.
- Unused budget does not carry over to the next calendar year.

### 2.2 What the budget may be used for

- Courses, workshops and online learning subscriptions.
- Conference and event registration fees.
- Books, including e-books and audiobooks.
- Professional certifications and exam fees.
- Membership of a professional body, where it is relevant to your role.
- Language courses, where the language is used in your work or is the language of a Fernhill office.

The learning must relate to your current role or to a role you could reasonably move into at Fernhill.

### 2.3 What the budget may not be used for

- Laptops, tablets, e-readers or any other hardware (POL-007 section 6.3).
- Degree programs. See tuition assistance in section 6.
- Training that Fernhill requires you to complete. Fernhill pays for required training separately (section 7).
- Travel, hotels and meals for a conference. These are business travel (section 4.2).
- Learning for a hobby or for a business of your own.
- Anything bought before the budget became available to you.

### 2.4 How to claim

Get approval in writing before you pay. Pay for the item yourself, then claim through the expense tool under the category "Learning" with the receipt and the approval. The normal expense deadline applies: submit within 30 calendar days of the date you paid (POL-007 section 2.3).

### 2.5 Spending that crosses a year end

An item is charged to the budget of the calendar year in which you paid for it, even if the course takes place in the following year.

### 2.6 Leaving Fernhill

The learning budget ends on your last day, and claims must be submitted before your last day (POL-009 section 11). The repayment rule for courses and certifications that cost more than USD 1,000 is in POL-009 section 7.3.

## 3. Study Leave

### 3.1 Entitlement

An employee who has completed 6 months of employment may take up to 3 paid days of study leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a prorated amount.

### 3.2 What study leave is for

Study leave is for sitting an exam, attending a course in person, or preparing for an exam in the 5 working days before it. The course or certification must have been approved under the learning budget rules or under tuition assistance.

### 3.3 Requesting study leave

Request study leave from your manager at least 10 business days in advance and record it in the HR portal. Study leave is separate from paid time off. It does not reduce your PTO balance, it does not carry over, and it is not paid out when employment ends.

## 4. Conferences

### 4.1 Attending

You may attend up to 2 external conferences per calendar year during working time, with your manager's approval. Attendance during working time is not PTO and is not study leave. Additional conferences may be approved by your department director when you are speaking on behalf of Fernhill (see POL-024 section 7).

### 4.2 Costs

The registration fee is charged to your learning budget. Travel, hotels and meals for an approved conference are business travel and are claimed under POL-007 section 4, from the department travel budget, within the normal travel limits. Booking rules are in the Business Travel Booking and Traveler Safety Procedure (POL-025).

### 4.3 Conferences in another country

Attending a conference abroad at Fernhill's request is business travel. It does not count toward the Work From Anywhere limit in POL-005. If you stay on to work remotely from that country after the conference, those working days do count (POL-007 section 4.5).

## 5. Internal Learning

### 5.1 Fernhill Learning Library

The Fernhill Learning Library is an online library of courses. It is free, it is available from your first day, and it does not use any of your learning budget. It is available to employees and interns.

### 5.2 Mentoring

The mentoring program matches an employee with a more experienced colleague outside their reporting line for 6 months. Applications open in February and August. Any employee who has completed the probationary period may apply to be a mentee. Mentors must have at least 2 years of employment at Fernhill.

### 5.3 Learning time

Each employee may spend up to 2 hours per week of working time on learning, by agreement with their manager. Learning time may not be saved up and taken as days off.

## 6. Tuition Assistance

### 6.1 What it is

Tuition assistance helps with the cost of a degree or a long professional qualification that is relevant to your career at Fernhill. It is separate from the learning budget.

### 6.2 Eligibility

You may apply if you are a full-time employee, you have completed 2 years of employment, your most recent performance rating is "Meets expectations" or higher (POL-015 section 4), and you are not on a performance improvement plan.

### 6.3 Amount

Fernhill reimburses up to USD 5,000 of tuition fees per calendar year, for up to 4 years. Books, travel and living costs are not covered.

### 6.4 Approval

Apply through your manager at least 60 calendar days before the program begins. Tuition assistance needs approval from your department director and from the Chief People Officer. The number of places each year is limited by budget.

### 6.5 Reimbursement

Fees are reimbursed after each term or module, when you show proof of payment and proof that you passed.

### 6.6 Repayment

If you leave Fernhill voluntarily within 12 months of receiving a tuition assistance payment, you must repay that payment in full, where local law allows. This rule is separate from the learning budget repayment rule in POL-009 section 7.3.

## 7. Required Training

Training that Fernhill requires, such as Security Awareness, Code of Conduct and Data Privacy training (POL-011 section 5), is provided free of charge and is completed during working time. It does not use the learning budget. A certification that your role requires by law or by customer contract is paid for by your department and does not use your learning budget.

## 8. Interns

Interns have access to the Fernhill Learning Library and to required training. Interns do not have a learning budget, study leave or tuition assistance. If an intern becomes an employee, the 6 month waiting period for the learning budget is measured from the original start date (POL-001 section 3.5).

## 9. Manager Responsibilities

Managers should discuss development at least twice a year, respond to a learning request within 5 business days, and give a reason when a request is declined. A request should not be declined only because the learning relates to a future role and not the current one.

## 10. What the HR Assistant Can and Cannot Do

The HR assistant can explain these rules, tell you whether your learning budget is available yet and show the amount used so far from your own record. It cannot approve a request or a claim. Approval always comes from the people named in this policy.

## 11. Questions

Raise a ticket under "Benefits Question" for questions about the learning budget and under "Policy Question" for anything else in this policy. See POL-013 for response times.
