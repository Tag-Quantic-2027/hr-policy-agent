# Paid Time Off Policy

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Paid time off (PTO) is time away from work for rest, travel or personal matters, paid at your normal rate. This policy explains who earns PTO, how much, how to request it and what happens to unused days.

This policy applies to full-time and part-time employees in all countries. It does not apply to interns or independent contractors, who do not accrue PTO (see POL-001 section 3).

Sick leave, parental leave, bereavement leave and other types of leave are separate from PTO and are covered by the Leave of Absence Policy (POL-010). Company holidays are covered by POL-003 and are not deducted from PTO.

Where local law gives an employee a greater entitlement than this policy, local law applies.

## 2. How Much PTO You Earn

### 2.1 Annual entitlement for full-time employees

| Completed years of service | PTO days per year |
|---|---|
| Less than 3 years | 15 days |
| 3 years to less than 6 years | 20 days |
| 6 years or more | 25 days |

A PTO day is 8 hours. The higher rate starts on the first day of the pay period after your service anniversary.

### 2.2 How PTO accrues

PTO accrues evenly across 24 semi-monthly pay periods. An employee entitled to 15 days per year accrues 0.625 days per pay period. PTO starts accruing on your first day of employment.

### 2.3 Part-time employees

Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their scheduled weekly hours. An employee scheduled for 24 hours per week accrues 60 percent of the full-time entitlement for their length of service. For example, with less than 3 years of service, that is 9 days per year.

### 2.4 Employees in Portugal

Employees whose country of employment is Portugal receive a minimum of 22 working days of annual leave per year, as required by local law, regardless of length of service. The 25 day rate applies after 6 years of service.

## 3. Requesting PTO

### 3.1 How to request

Submit a PTO request through the HR portal as a ticket in the category "PTO Request". The request must include the first and last day of the absence and the number of working days requested. Your manager approves or declines the request.

### 3.2 Notice periods

The amount of notice you must give depends on how many consecutive working days you are requesting:

| Length of absence | Minimum notice before the first day off | Approval required |
|---|---|---|
| 1 to 2 working days | 3 business days | Manager |
| 3 to 5 working days | 10 business days | Manager |
| More than 5 consecutive working days | 20 business days | Manager and department director |

Business days exclude weekends and the company holidays for your country of employment (see POL-003).

### 3.3 Short-notice requests

A request that does not meet the notice period is a short-notice request. Short-notice requests are not automatically refused. Your manager may approve a short-notice request as an exception if team coverage allows. You should state the reason for the short notice in the request. A manager is not required to give a reason for declining a short-notice request.

### 3.4 Manager response time

Managers must respond to a PTO request within 3 business days. If you have had no response after 3 business days, contact People Operations, who will follow up with the manager. A request is not approved until the manager has approved it in the HR portal. Do not book non-refundable travel before approval.

### 3.5 Employees with no assigned manager

If your profile shows no assigned manager, for example because your manager has recently left, send the request to People Operations, who will route it to the acting manager.

## 4. Balance Rules

### 4.1 Checking your balance

Your available balance is shown in the HR portal. The available balance is your accrued days minus days already taken and minus days in pending or approved future requests.

### 4.2 Requesting more than your balance

You may request PTO only up to your available balance, with one exception. After the probationary period, a manager may approve a request that takes your balance negative by up to 3 days. A negative balance is repaid by future accruals. No further PTO can be approved while the balance is negative, other than as required by law.

### 4.3 Negative balance at the end of employment

If employment ends while the balance is negative, the value of the negative balance is deducted from final pay where local law allows.

## 5. PTO During the Probationary Period

PTO accrues from the first day of employment. However, PTO cannot be taken during the first 60 calendar days of employment unless both the manager and People Operations approve an exception in writing. Exceptions are normally granted only for commitments that the employee disclosed before accepting the offer.

From day 61 to day 90, PTO may be taken with manager approval, up to the accrued balance. A negative balance is not permitted during the probationary period.

## 6. Blackout Periods

Some teams have periods when PTO is not normally approved because of predictable peak workload:

- Finance department: the last 10 business days of each fiscal quarter.
- Sales department: the last 5 business days of each fiscal quarter.

Fiscal quarters end on March 31, June 30, September 30 and December 31. During a blackout period, PTO requires approval from the department director in addition to the manager, whatever the length of the absence. The winter office closure described in POL-003 is not affected by blackout periods.

## 7. Carryover and Expiry

### 7.1 Carryover limit

You may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days from one calendar year to the next. Unused days above 5 are forfeited on December 31, except where local law requires otherwise.

### 7.2 Expiry of carried-over days

Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the following year. Carried-over days that are not used by March 31 expire. Carried-over days are used first when you take PTO.

### 7.3 Employees in Portugal

For employees in Portugal, statutory leave that cannot be taken in the year it is earned is carried over as required by local law, and must be taken by April 30 of the following year.

## 8. PTO and Other Absences

### 8.1 Illness during PTO

If you become ill during PTO, you may ask to convert the affected days to sick leave. A doctor's note is required. See POL-010 section 2.

### 8.2 Company holidays during PTO

A company holiday that falls during your PTO is not deducted from your PTO balance.

### 8.3 PTO combined with working from another location

PTO days are not working days. Days of PTO taken while you are abroad do not count toward the Work From Anywhere limit in POL-005. Only days on which you work count.

## 9. Cancelling or Changing PTO

You may cancel or change approved PTO at any time before it starts by updating the ticket in the HR portal. Cancelled days are returned to your balance. In rare cases a manager may ask you to move approved PTO because of an urgent business need. If you agree, Fernhill reimburses reasonable non-refundable costs under POL-007. You are not required to agree.

## 10. PTO at the End of Employment

Accrued and unused PTO is paid out in the final pay, up to a maximum of 10 days, unless local law requires a higher payout. Carried-over days that have already expired are not paid out.

## 11. Questions

Questions about your balance should be raised as a ticket in the category "PTO Request". Questions about how this policy applies to an unusual situation should be raised under "Policy Question". See POL-013 for response times.
