# Offboarding and Separation Policy

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy explains what happens when employment at Fernhill ends: how to resign, the notice period, the last weeks of work, final pay, benefits, equipment, references and returning to Fernhill in the future. It applies to full-time employees, part-time employees and interns. The end of a contractor's engagement is governed by the contract.

Several of the rules that apply at the end of employment are set in other policies. This policy brings them together and cites the policy that sets each rule. If this policy and the cited policy ever appear to disagree, the cited policy applies to its own subject.

The HR assistant can explain this policy in general terms. Questions about an individual's dismissal, redundancy, severance or dispute are handled only by People Operations staff (POL-013 section 5.2).

## 2. Resignation

### 2.1 How to resign

Tell your manager, then confirm your resignation in writing by submitting the resignation form in the HR portal. The form records the date you gave notice and your proposed last day. A resignation is not recorded until the form is submitted. A message to your manager in chat is not enough.

### 2.2 Notice period

Fernhill asks for the following notice:

| Role | Notice requested |
|---|---|
| Job levels L1 to L4 | 2 weeks |
| Job levels L5 and above, and all people managers | 4 weeks |
| Interns | 1 week |

If your employment contract or local law sets a different notice period, the contract or the law applies. This is normally the case for employees in Portugal and often the case for employees in Canada. People Operations confirms your notice period and last day in writing within 2 business days of the form being submitted.

### 2.3 Withdrawing a resignation

You may withdraw a resignation within 2 business days of submitting the form, and it will be accepted. After 2 business days, a withdrawal needs the agreement of your manager and department director.

### 2.4 Shortening the notice period

Fernhill may agree to an earlier last day at your request. Fernhill may also decide that you should not work during some or all of the notice period. In that case you are paid for the notice period as normal.

## 3. During the Notice Period

### 3.1 Handover

Agree a handover plan with your manager in the first 3 business days of the notice period. The plan lists your current work, who takes over each item, and where documents are stored.

### 3.2 Paid time off during the notice period

PTO during the notice period needs your manager's approval and is normally approved only if the handover will still be completed. PTO that was approved before you resigned stays approved unless you and your manager agree to change it. You may not use PTO to bring your last day forward without your manager's agreement.

### 3.3 Sick leave during the notice period

Sick leave during the notice period needs a doctor's note for any number of days. Send it to People Operations, not to your manager (POL-010 section 2.4).

### 3.4 Conduct and confidentiality

All policies continue to apply until your last day. You may not copy, send or keep Fernhill information for use after you leave (POL-006 section 10). Customer lists, source code and pricing remain confidential after employment ends.

## 4. The Exit Interview

People Operations offers every departing employee an exit interview in the last week of employment. The interview is voluntary. What you say is shared with leadership only in summary and without your name, unless you raise a matter that must be investigated, such as harassment or a safety concern (POL-012).

## 5. Last Day Checklist

| Item | Rule | Policy |
|---|---|---|
| System access | Removed at the end of your last day | POL-006 section 10 |
| Company equipment | Returned within 10 business days of your last day | POL-008 section 8.1 |
| Building pass | Returned on your last day in the office, or with the equipment | POL-008 section 8 |
| Company credit card | Cancelled on your last day. All transactions must be matched to receipts before then | POL-007 section 9 |
| Expense claims | Submitted before your last day where possible, and in any case within 30 calendar days of the expense | POL-007 section 2.3 |
| Wellness allowance and learning budget claims | Submitted before your last day | POL-009 section 11 |
| Personal contact details | Updated in the HR portal so that tax forms reach you | POL-014 section 12 |

Hybrid and fully remote employees receive a prepaid shipping box from IT. Items bought with the home office stipend belong to you and are not returned (POL-008 section 8.2).

## 6. Final Pay

### 6.1 When it is paid

Final pay is paid on the next regular pay date after your last day (POL-014 section 3.1), or earlier where local law requires.

### 6.2 What it includes

- Pay for all time worked up to and including your last day.
- Accrued and unused PTO, up to a maximum of 10 days, unless local law requires a higher payout. Carried-over days that have already expired are not paid out (POL-002 section 10).
- Approved overtime and on-call allowances not yet paid (POL-014 section 7).
- Approved expense claims not yet paid.

### 6.3 What is not paid out

- Unused sick leave (POL-010 section 2.5).
- Unused floating holidays (POL-003 section 6).
- Unused study leave and time off in lieu, unless local law requires it (POL-016 section 3.3 and POL-017 section 8).
- An annual bonus, if you leave or give notice before the payment date, unless local law requires it (POL-014 section 6.3).

### 6.4 What may be deducted

Where local law allows, the following may be deducted from final pay:

- The value of a negative PTO balance (POL-002 section 4.3).
- A repayment due under the learning budget rule (POL-009 section 7.3), the tuition assistance rule (POL-016 section 6.6) or the relocation assistance rule (POL-022 section 7.4).
- The outstanding amount of a hardship advance (POL-014 section 10).
- Personal spending on a company credit card that has not been repaid (POL-007 section 9).

People Operations sends you a written statement of final pay, including each deduction and the policy it is based on, at least 2 business days before the payment.

## 7. Benefits After You Leave

- Health, dental and vision coverage ends on the last day of the month in which employment ends. Information about continuing coverage at your own cost is sent with your final pay details (POL-009 section 11).
- Life and disability insurance end on your last day.
- Your own retirement plan contributions are always yours. Employer match contributions that have not vested are forfeited. Vesting is one third after each completed year of service (POL-009 section 5.3).
- The Employee Assistance Program remains available to you and your household for 30 calendar days after your last day.

## 8. Equipment Not Returned

If equipment has not been returned within 10 business days, People Operations contacts you. If it has still not been returned after 30 calendar days, Fernhill may recover the current value of the equipment where local law allows (POL-008 section 8.3).

## 9. References and Proof of Employment

### 9.1 What Fernhill confirms

People Operations confirms dates of employment and final job title to a third party on request. Pay is confirmed only with your written consent (POL-020 section 7).

### 9.2 Personal references

A manager may give a personal reference in their own name. A manager may not give a reference on Fernhill letterhead or present a personal opinion as the view of Fernhill. Requests for an official reference go to People Operations.

## 10. When Fernhill Ends the Employment

### 10.1 Who decides

A decision to end someone's employment is never taken by a manager alone. People Operations reviews every case before the employee is told, and the Chief People Officer approves any case involving a director or above.

### 10.2 Notice and severance

Notice and any severance are set by the employment contract and local law, and are confirmed to the employee in writing. This policy does not set severance amounts.

### 10.3 Role of the HR assistant

The HR assistant does not discuss why a person's employment is ending, what they may be owed, or whether a decision was fair. It explains the general rules in this policy and directs the person to People Operations. The former employee keeps read access to their final pay statement and tax forms through the alumni page of the HR portal for 12 months.

## 11. Retirement

An employee who plans to retire is asked to give 3 months of notice where possible, so that a successor can be found. All other rules in this policy apply in the same way.

## 12. Returning to Fernhill

### 12.1 Eligibility for rehire

A former employee who resigned, gave the notice requested and returned all equipment is eligible for rehire. Rehiring follows the normal hiring process in the Hiring, Referrals and Internal Mobility Policy (POL-021).

### 12.2 Service after rehire

Rules on how earlier service is counted after rehire are in POL-021 section 9.

## 13. Interns

An internship ends on the date in the internship agreement and no resignation is needed. Interns return equipment on their last day or within 10 business days if they work remotely. Interns do not accrue PTO, so no PTO is paid out (POL-001 section 3.3).

## 14. Questions

General questions may be raised as a ticket under "Policy Question". Questions about final pay go under "Payroll Question". For anything about the end of your own employment, contact People Operations directly at people@fernhill.example.
