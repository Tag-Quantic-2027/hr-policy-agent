# Relocation and International Transfer Policy

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy covers lasting changes to where you are based: a change of assigned office within your country, a permanent move to another Fernhill country, and the financial help Fernhill gives when it asks you to move. It applies to full-time and part-time employees. Interns and independent contractors are not covered.

This policy is about permanent moves. It does not cover:

- Working temporarily from another state, province or country. That is the Work From Anywhere program (POL-005).
- Moving your home to another state or province while keeping the same role and office. That is covered by POL-004 section 6.3, summarized in section 3 below.
- Business trips (POL-007 and POL-025).

## 2. Which Rule Applies to You

| What you want to do | Rule |
|---|---|
| Work from somewhere else for a few days or weeks and come back | POL-005 |
| Move home within your country, same role | POL-004 section 6.3 and section 3 of this policy |
| Change your assigned office within your country | Section 4 |
| Move permanently to another Fernhill country | Sections 5 and 6 |
| Live and work permanently in a country where Fernhill has no office | Not possible. See section 8 |

## 3. Moving Home Within Your Country

Raise a ticket under "Work Arrangement Change" at least 30 calendar days before the move. A move to a registered state or province is normally approved. A move to a state or province that is not registered needs a tax and payroll review, which can take up to 60 calendar days, and may be declined (POL-004 section 6.3). Your pay may change if the new location is in a different pay zone (POL-014 section 2.3). Fernhill does not pay the cost of a move you choose to make.

Do not move first and tell People Operations afterward. Working from a state or province where Fernhill is not registered, without approval, is a breach of POL-004.

## 4. Changing Your Assigned Office Within Your Country

A change of assigned office, for example from the Denver office to fully remote in Texas, is a change of work arrangement. It follows POL-004 section 4.2: a ticket under "Work Arrangement Change", approval from your manager and department director, and 15 business days for a decision.

## 5. International Transfers: Eligibility

An international transfer is a permanent change of your country of employment to another country where Fernhill has an office: the United States, Canada or Portugal. You may request one if all of the following are true:

- You are a full-time or part-time employee and have completed at least 18 months of employment.
- Your most recent performance rating is "Meets expectations" or higher (POL-015 section 4).
- You are not on a performance improvement plan (POL-015 section 6.3).
- There is an open role for you in the destination country, or your current role can be performed from there and both department directors agree.
- You have, or can obtain, the right to work in the destination country.

Meeting these conditions makes you eligible to ask. It does not guarantee approval.

## 6. International Transfers: Process

### 6.1 Request and approval

Raise a ticket under "Work Arrangement Change" at least 90 calendar days before the date you would like to move. The transfer needs approval from the department director in your current country, the department director in the destination country and the Chief People Officer. People Operations gives a decision within 30 calendar days of the ticket.

### 6.2 Immigration

People Operations works with an immigration adviser to confirm whether you have the right to work in the destination country and what permit is needed. Fernhill pays the adviser and the permit fees for the employee. The HR assistant does not answer questions about an individual's immigration status or work authorization (POL-013 section 5.2). It can explain this policy and prepare a draft ticket.

A transfer cannot start until the right to work is confirmed in writing. Permit processing times are outside Fernhill's control, and the 90 calendar days of notice is a minimum.

### 6.3 New contract

You receive a new employment contract with the Fernhill company in the destination country. Your employment in the first country ends on the day before the new contract begins, with no gap. Your original start date is kept as your service date.

### 6.4 What changes on transfer

| Item | What happens |
|---|---|
| Pay | Set from the pay band for your level in the destination country, in local currency (POL-014 section 2) |
| Pay dates | Follow the destination country (POL-014 section 3.1) |
| Company holidays | Follow the destination country from the transfer date (POL-003 section 2) |
| PTO entitlement | Follows POL-002 for the destination country, using your service date for tenure |
| PTO balance | Your available balance in days is carried over in full. The carryover limit and expiry date of the destination country then apply |
| Benefits | The benefits of the first country end and those of the destination country begin, with no waiting period for health coverage (POL-009 section 9) |
| Retirement plan | Your account in the first country stays where it is. Vesting service continues from your service date |
| Probationary period | Does not apply again |
| Data access | Reviewed. A role with Restricted data access must be performed from the country of employment (POL-006 section 3.4) |
| Equipment | You keep your laptop if IT confirms it can be supported in the destination country. Otherwise it is exchanged (POL-008 section 7.2) |

### 6.5 Tax

A change of country has personal tax consequences. Fernhill pays for one session with a tax adviser in each country for the year of the move. Your personal tax remains your responsibility.

## 7. Relocation Assistance

### 7.1 When it is offered

Relocation assistance is offered only when Fernhill asks you to move, or when you are hired into a role that requires you to move. If you ask to move for your own reasons, Fernhill pays the immigration costs in section 6.2 and the tax sessions in section 6.5, and nothing else.

### 7.2 Amounts

| Type of move | Maximum relocation assistance |
|---|---|
| Within a country, more than 80 kilometers (50 miles) | USD 8,000 |
| Between countries | USD 15,000 |

In addition, Fernhill pays for temporary housing for up to 30 calendar days at the destination, within the hotel limits in POL-007 section 4.2.

### 7.3 What it covers and how to claim

Relocation assistance reimburses actual costs against receipts: moving and storing household goods, one-way travel for you and your household, and fees for ending a lease early. It does not cover the purchase or sale of a home, furniture, or losses on a vehicle. Claim through the expense tool under the category "Relocation" within 6 months of the transfer date. The approval limits in POL-007 section 3 apply.

### 7.4 Repayment

If you leave Fernhill voluntarily after receiving relocation assistance, you must repay it as follows, where local law allows:

- Within 6 months of the transfer date: all of it.
- More than 6 months and up to 12 months after the transfer date: half of it.
- More than 12 months after the transfer date: nothing.

Any amount due may be deducted from final pay (POL-018 section 6.4).

### 7.5 Home office stipend

The home office stipend is a one-time payment (POL-007 section 6.1). A relocation does not give you a second stipend.

## 8. Countries Where Fernhill Has No Office

Fernhill can employ people only in the United States, Canada and Portugal. It is not possible to be based permanently in any other country and remain a Fernhill employee. An employee who wants to spend time in another country may use the Work From Anywhere program, within the limit of 20 working days per calendar year and the other rules in POL-005. Exceptions above that limit are rare and are never approved for more than 40 working days in a calendar year (POL-005 section 5.3).

## 9. Short-Term Assignments Abroad

Fernhill may ask an employee to work from another Fernhill office for up to 90 calendar days for a business reason. A short-term assignment is business travel. It is arranged by People Operations, who confirm the right to work, tax and insurance. Travel and accommodation are paid by Fernhill. The days do not count toward the Work From Anywhere limit, because the work is at Fernhill's request. Your country of employment, pay, holidays and benefits do not change. An employee with a Restricted data access level may take a short-term assignment only if access to Restricted data is removed for its duration.

## 10. Family Members

Fernhill pays the permit fees for a spouse or partner and for dependent children when it has asked you to move. Fernhill does not find employment for a spouse or partner and does not pay school fees.

## 11. If the Transfer Does Not Go Ahead

If a permit is refused, or the open role is withdrawn before you move, you stay in your current role and country. Fernhill reimburses reasonable non-refundable costs that you incurred after the transfer was approved in writing. Do not sell a home, end a lease or book a move before you have the approval and the right to work in writing.

## 12. Questions

Raise a ticket under "Work Arrangement Change". See POL-013 for response times.
