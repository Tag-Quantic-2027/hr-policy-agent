Leave of Absence Policy

Fernhill Technologies is a fictional company created for a course project. Every person, office and figure in this document is invented.

1. Purpose and Scope

This policy describes the types of leave available at Fernhill Technologies other than paid time off and company holidays. Paid time off is covered by POL-002 and company holidays by POL-003.

This policy applies to full-time and part-time employees. Interns receive sick leave and bereavement leave only. Independent contractors are not covered by this policy.

Where local law gives an employee a greater entitlement than this policy, local law applies. Employees in Canada and Portugal have statutory leave rights that differ from the rules below. People Operations confirms the entitlement for each request.

2. Sick Leave

2.1 Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a prorated amount based on scheduled hours. Interns receive 1 day of paid sick leave for each full month of their internship. Sick leave is available from the first day of employment and is not affected by the probationary period.

2.2 What sick leave is for

Sick leave may be used for your own illness, injury or medical appointments, and to care for a sick child, partner or parent.

2.3 How to report

Tell your manager as early as possible on the first day of absence, and before your normal start time where you can. Record the absence in the HR portal when you return. You do not need to tell your manager the nature of the illness.

2.4 Doctor's note

A doctor's note is required for an absence of more than 3 consecutive working days. Send the note to People Operations, not to your manager. Medical information is Restricted data under POL-006 and is seen only by People Operations.

2.5 Unused sick leave

Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next year and is not paid out at the end of employment.

2.6 Illness during paid time off

If you fall ill during approved PTO, you may ask to convert the affected days to sick leave. A doctor's note is required for any number of days in this case.

3. Medical Leave

If illness or injury keeps you from work for more than 10 consecutive working days, the absence becomes medical leave. Medical leave is managed individually by a People Operations case manager, who explains your pay, your benefits and how short-term and long-term disability insurance apply.

The HR assistant does not answer questions about an individual's medical leave. It will direct you to People Operations and can prepare a draft ticket in the category "Leave Request" for you to confirm. See POL-013 section 5.

4. Parental Leave

4.1 Entitlement

An employee who becomes a parent through birth, adoption or foster placement receives 16 weeks of parental leave at full pay. The entitlement is the same for every parent, whatever their gender and whether or not they are the birth parent.

4.2 Eligibility

You are eligible for paid parental leave once you have completed 6 months of employment before the expected date of birth or placement. An employee with less than 6 months of employment receives the leave required by local law, which may be unpaid or paid at a lower rate.

4.3 Taking the leave

Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement. It may be taken in one block or in two blocks, each of at least 4 weeks. Tell your manager and raise a ticket in the category "Leave Request" at least 60 calendar days before the leave is expected to begin, where possible.

4.4 Returning to work

You return to the same role or an equivalent one. For the first 4 weeks after returning, you may work 80 percent of your normal hours at full pay.

5. Bereavement Leave

Employees and interns receive paid bereavement leave as follows:

  • Up to 5 days for the death of an immediate family member: a spouse or partner, child, parent, sibling, or a partner's parent.
  • Up to 2 days for the death of an extended family member: a grandparent, grandchild, aunt, uncle, niece, nephew or cousin.

Bereavement leave does not need advance notice. Tell your manager as soon as you can. No proof is normally required. If you need more time, speak to your manager about using PTO or unpaid personal leave.

6. Jury Duty and Civic Leave

Employees called for jury duty or required to appear as a witness receive full pay for up to 10 working days per calendar year. Give your manager a copy of the summons. Employees also receive up to 2 hours of paid time to vote in a national, state or provincial election if their working hours make voting difficult.

7. Unpaid Personal Leave

7.1 Entitlement

An employee who has completed 1 year of employment may request unpaid personal leave of up to 12 weeks in any rolling 24 month period, for reasons such as extended travel, study or family commitments.

7.2 Approval

Unpaid personal leave of up to 2 weeks needs approval from your manager and department director. Unpaid personal leave of more than 2 weeks also needs approval from a vice president. Raise a ticket in the category "Leave Request" at least 30 calendar days before the leave begins. Approval depends on business needs and is not guaranteed.

7.3 Pay and benefits during unpaid leave

You are not paid during unpaid personal leave, and PTO does not accrue. Health, dental and vision coverage continues for unpaid leave of up to 4 weeks, with Fernhill continuing its contribution. For unpaid leave longer than 4 weeks, you pay the full cost of coverage from week 5.

7.4 Use PTO first

You must use your available PTO balance before unpaid personal leave begins, except that you may keep up to 5 days.

8. Leave and Your Equipment

If you are on leave for more than 12 weeks, IT may ask you to return your company laptop for the period of the leave (POL-008 section 8.4). You should not work while on leave. Access to some systems is paused during leave of more than 4 weeks and is restored on your return.

9. Leave During the Probationary Period

Sick leave and bereavement leave are available during the probationary period. Leave of more than 5 consecutive working days during the probationary period extends the probationary period by the length of the leave.

10. Confidentiality

The reason for a leave request is shared only with those who need to know. Your manager is told the dates of your leave. Your manager is not told medical details. People Operations stores leave records as Confidential data and medical documents as Restricted data.

11. Questions

Raise a ticket in the HR portal under the category "Leave Request". For anything involving a medical condition, contact People Operations directly at people@fernhill.example. See POL-013 for response times.
